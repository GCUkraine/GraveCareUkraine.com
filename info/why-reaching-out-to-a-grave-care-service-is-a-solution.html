Why Reaching Out to a Grave Care Service Can Be the Easier Solution

Not sure who to ask to care for a family grave in Ukraine? A grave care service means no awkward price talk, and clear rules.

Grave Care Services Grave Care Ukraine

Someone from your family or your ancestors is buried in Ukraine. It can be a parent, or a great-grandmother you never met. You can't visit, or you haven't visited for a long time. You know the grave needs care, but you don't know who to ask.

If you are wondering how to arrange grave care from abroad, it looks like a simple task.

The Question Is Not Only "Who Can Go?"

Often there is someone. A cousin in Kyiv, an old friend, a neighbour of your parents. You can ask them.

But asking a person you know is not simple. They may say yes because it is hard to say no. You don't know how much to offer. You may worry what they think when they see the grave after so many years. And they have their own life, their own work and their own free time.

None of this makes them bad people. It just makes the favour heavier than it looks.

Why Talking About Price With Friends Gets Awkward

We know this from the beginning — we have been doing this since 2008. Back then, friends who had moved abroad asked us to visit a grave, clean it, and take a photo. They wanted to send money, but they didn't know how much. We were not sure about taking it. In the end, we agreed to cover only the transport costs.

For one visit, that was fine. For regular care, it doesn't work well. Every time, someone has to remind, ask again, and think about who owes what to whom.

Money between friends and relatives is never only about money. That is why many people feel awkward even to start the conversation.

What Changes When You Work With a Service

With a service, the rules are clear before anything starts. Our prices are open on our website, and the work is described in detail. You know what we do, how we take photos, and how we talk with you.

We keep track of the dates too — but only the way you approve. For regular care, we send a reminder in spring, or in the months you choose, or before a date that matters to you. For yearly cleaning, we do the cleaning and remind you in spring to check that everything is still on. Most of our clients tell us this is very convenient.

Some of our clients choose a service exactly for this. They pay, they know the work will be done as described, and they don't depend on anyone. For them it feels almost like cleaning the grave themselves — only from a distance. They don't need to feel awkward about money, and they don't need to ask for a favour.

If you want to see what is behind the price, read what the price of grave care includes.

We Don't Judge, and We Don't Ask Why

Maybe it has been many years since anyone visited. You may feel that you need to explain why.

You don't. The grave can belong to a parent, a relative, or someone you knew and now want to take care of. For us it doesn't matter. We don't need a reason. Cleaning a grave is something we can do without questions, and we do it carefully.

If you want to tell us more — who this person was, or why you decided to do it now — we are happy to listen. It is fine either way. You want the grave cleaned, and we clean it.

How to Say What You Want — and What You Don't Like

Tell us your wishes in your own words. You don't need the right wording or the right order. We wrote more about this in how we arrange grave care from abroad.

The same is true for the harder part: things you don't like. Maybe a photo shows a spot we missed. Maybe you wanted something done differently. Tell us plainly. We want to hear it, and we will talk about it with you.

With relatives, you cannot always say it straight. You need to find the right words, or maybe stay quiet. You may not want to offend someone who did you a favour, or hear critics from someone who thinks you should have come earlier.

With a company, it is different. You pay for the work, and we know it, so we just do our work. Usually there are no frictions or issues. If you want something different, or some extra work, we are happy to hear you. We have our standards, and they cover what we do for the fixed price.

What to Expect When You Request a Cleaning

You tell us what you know — a city, a cemetery, a name. Even a little is enough.

We ask what we need, and we tell you honestly what we can do. You hear the price before you agree to anything. Writing to us and talking about your case costs nothing.

After the visit, you get photos and a short report. You can see the result yourself. You can decide after that if you need one visit or regular care.

Whenever You Are Ready

With some of our clients, after some time of working together, we stop being only a service. We become friends, or something like very distant relatives, and we stay people they can easily talk to about the grave and about any other request they have in Ukraine. The awkward talk about money is gone. There is no fear that we will judge them as a son, a daughter or a grandchild, because the grave was not cared for during many years before they came to us. They can say freely what they like and what they don't like, and they know exactly what to expect.

We are not a corporate company, and we don't want to sound like one. In our communication we are human and friendly. But the rules stay clear, and so do our standards of service. That is why it works.

You don't need the right words to start. Tell us what you know. One message is enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

I feel a bit awkward. My mother's grave is overgrown after so many years without care. How do I ask someone to do this?

Just write to us. No worries, we will take care of it. No questions, no judgment. We are just doing our job, and we are happy you decided to start taking care of the grave of someone you love.

Should I think about the price?

No. Our prices are open, and there is a list of services for a fixed price. You can see what is included. The prices are reasonable: they cover the trip to the cemetery, enough time on site, and doing the cleaning properly.

Some things are extra, and the price depends on the case. For example, removing a big tree depends on how hard it is to remove. Painting a fence depends on its size in square metres. Extra work like this is priced and agreed with you before it starts. The fixed prices cover the services in the list.

Can I tell you if I don't like something?

Yes, of course. Say it plainly. If you want something done differently, or you want extra work, we are happy to hear you and talk about it.

I'm not sure I can keep this up every year. Do I have to commit?

No. Some people order one visit, and some choose annual care. You can decide after you have seen the first result.
